# Privacy Policy

**B.I.R.X. — Reclaim the Memories**<br>
**Developer:** nomadcz<br>
**Privacy contact:** nomad@centrum.cz<br>
**Effective date:** 5 November 2026

This policy applies to the game **B.I.R.X.** distributed on Google Play and Steam.

## The short version

B.I.R.X. does not collect, transmit, sell, rent or share personal data. The game stores your
progress and settings in a local save file on your device. There are no accounts, ads, analytics,
tracking, crash reporting or in-app purchases.

## What the game stores

- Game progress (level, score, lives and similar run state).
- Game options (for example sound, display and difficulty settings).

These are written to a save file in the app's local data folder. They stay on your device and are
not sent to us or to anyone else.

## What the game does not collect

The game itself does not collect or access:

- your name, email address, phone number or contacts;
- your location;
- photos, camera or microphone;
- advertising identifiers or other device identifiers;
- analytics, crash reports or usage statistics;
- account information (the game has no accounts).

The Android build requests no permissions. The game itself does not connect to the internet or send
data to us.

## Sharing

We do not sell, rent, trade or share personal data. We have no server that receives game data.

Google Play and Steam are independent platforms. They may collect information about installs,
purchases, devices or usage under their own privacy policies. That processing is controlled by
Google or Valve, not by this game.

## Retention and deletion

The local save file remains on your device until you delete it. You can remove it by clearing the
app's data (Android), deleting the game's local save files (PC), or uninstalling the game. We do
not hold a copy, so there is nothing for us to delete on a server.

If you email us, we receive your email address and message. We keep it only as long as needed to
answer you, then delete it unless we are required to keep it by law.

## Children

The game is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from
anyone, including children. Because the game collects no data, there is no child-specific data to
delete.

## Security

The game does not transmit personal data, so there is no data in transit to protect. The local save
file is protected by your device's normal app sandbox and any device encryption or backup settings
you use.

## Changes

If the game's data practices change (for example if a future version adds online features, analytics
or crash reporting), we will update this page and the in-game privacy notice before those features
are released. The effective date at the top will change.
